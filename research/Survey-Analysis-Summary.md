# Speak Back Survey Analysis

Cleaned survey base: **53 responses**. Two Google Forms were merged; 55 responses were received and 53 retained after cleaning. The sample comes from the author's network and is directional, not representative.

## Respondent status
- Active: 22 of 53
- Lapsed: 18 of 53
- At-risk (thinking of stopping): 13 of 53

## Speaking confidence
- Conversation self-rating 1/5: 11 of 53
- Conversation self-rating 2/5: 19 of 53
- Conversation self-rating 3/5: 17 of 53
- Conversation self-rating 4/5: 6 of 53
- Conversation self-rating 5/5: 0 of 53
- Low self-rating (1 or 2): 30 of 53
- Average self-rating: 2.34 out of 5

Base: 51 responses.
- Would like it: 25 of 51
- Neutral: 12 of 51

## Reasons for stopping or considering stopping
Base: 31 lapsed or at-risk respondents; respondents could select multiple reasons.
- No time: 13 of 31
- Got bored: 11 of 31
- Did not feel improvement: 10 of 31
- Wanted real conversation: 8 of 31
- Lost streak: 5 of 31

## Interpretation limits
These are self-reported responses from a small convenience sample. They do not establish causality, represent Duolingo's user base, or prove demand at scale. Use the denominators shown above; bases vary by question.
