# Speak Back — Research Appendix

Companion to the Speak Back PRD. This repository copy documents the research protocol and reporting rules for the independent portfolio project.

## Research status
Survey findings are included in PRD v0.3. Interviews with real participants have not yet been completed. Do not treat practice answers, hypothetical personas or interview-preparation notes as collected research.

## Reporting rules
- Report counts as “X of N”; include the denominator.
- Treat this small, network-based sample as directional, not proof.
- State who was reached: respondents are primarily from the author's own network.
- Avoid percentage claims for segments with fewer than 10 people.
- Separate observed findings from assumptions and targets.

## Interview plan
Target 5–6 interviews:
- 2 active Duolingo learners
- 2 lapsed Duolingo learners
- 1–2 users of other speaking or language-exchange apps

Ask about actual past behaviour before describing Speak Back. Introduce the concept only near the end, to avoid leading participants.

## Interview prompts
1. Tell me about the last time you used Duolingo.
2. What usually makes you open it?
3. Have you tried speaking the language outside the app? Tell me about the most recent time.
4. What happened when you tried to speak?
5. What do you currently do when you want speaking practice?
6. Have you ever practised with a friend or another learner? What was that experience like?
7. After exploring the Speak Back concept, what would make you try it or avoid it?

## Analysis
For each interview, capture the participant type, recent behaviour, pain points, workarounds, motivation, concerns and notable quotes. Group repeated themes and report the number of interviewees who raised each theme. Do not convert illustrative or practice responses into participant findings.

## Survey source and cleaning
The survey workbook records 55 merged responses and 53 cleaned responses. Cleaning included removing two duplicate submissions, normalizing text formatting and blanking invalid shifted answers rather than guessing. Identical responses on different days and some inconsistent conditional answers were flagged but retained. Refer to the cleaned analysis for exact handling and denominators.
