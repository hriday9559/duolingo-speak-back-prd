# Speak Back PRD — v0.3 status

The current working PRD is Draft v0.3, last updated 19 November 2026. It is the master PRD for this portfolio project.

## Concept
Speak Back proposes short, prompted asynchronous voice replies between mutual Duolingo friends. It aims to connect real-person speaking practice with an existing social habit, without requiring live calls or stranger matching.

## Current survey evidence
- 30 of 53 respondents rated their ability to hold a simple two-minute conversation as 1 or 2 out of 5.
- 25 of 51 said they would like to practice with another learner.
- 29 of 51 preferred a friend format, compared with 9 of 51 who selected a stranger format.
- 23 of 51 selected voice-message formats; 15 of 51 selected live-call formats.

The survey combines two Google Forms collected over approximately six months. There were 55 merged responses and 53 retained after cleaning. Respondents came from the author's network; results are directional and not representative of Duolingo's user base.

## Current product decision
Option A: asynchronous prompted voice messages with mutual friends. The PRD treats reach, impact, confidence, effort, targets, personas and RICE values as assumptions where indicated.

## Research status
The survey is complete for this draft. Interviews have not yet been conducted with real participants. The interview script belongs in the research appendix. Interview-preparation material is private and must not be presented as real participant evidence.

## Scope highlights
P0 includes short prompted voice replies, thread-based replies, course-level prompts and hints, mutual-friend-only access, safety controls, screening and analytics. Stranger matching and live calls are later-phase ideas, not MVP scope.
